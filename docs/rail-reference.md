# Bangkok rail reference

Verified 2026-10-20 against the operators' published data:

- [BTS official route map](https://www.bts.co.th/eng/routemap.html): English station names/order from its station-pointer data, including N24–E23 and W1–S12. [BTS structure and stations](https://www.bts.co.th/eng/library/system-structuer.html) independently confirms 47 Sukhumvit and 14 Silom stops (Siam shared; 60 unique BTS stations). N6 is not an operating station. Remove an accidental Thai combining mark from the official English Wongwian Yai label.
- [BEM official Blue station selector](https://metro.bemplc.co.th/Line-Maps?Line=1&lang=en): all 38 station names in BL01–BL38 order. [BEM system map](https://metro.bemplc.co.th/MRT-System-Map?lang=en) confirms the complete 38-station network and links the official route diagram.

Within the three supported lines, interchanges are Siam (shared BTS node), Mo Chit/Chatuchak Park, Ha Yaek Lat Phrao/Phahon Yothin, Asok/Sukhumvit, Sala Daeng/Si Lom and Bang Wa. Transfers to out-of-scope lines are deliberately omitted.

Blue is a loop Tha Phra → Charan 13 → … → Itsaraphap → Tha Phra, with a branch Tha Phra → Bang Phai → Bang Wa → … → Lak Song. Itsaraphap does **not** connect directly to Bang Phai. Keep the two operators' Bang Wa stations and Asok/Sukhumvit identities separate, linked by interchange connectors. Correct the prototype's BL33/BL34 omission/misnumbering.

The schematic keeps the prototype's central geometry, river and line colors. Outer BTS stations extend along the official map's bearings; coordinates are diagram units, not geography or walking routes. Reference data ships statically, separate from mutable/private offers. Names appear without station codes; IDs remain stable for later persisted station assignments.
